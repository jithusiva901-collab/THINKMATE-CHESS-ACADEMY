About Thinkmate Chess Academy

“More than chess—we build confident thinkers and future champions.” ♟️

Why Choose Thinkmate?

At Thinkmate Chess Academy, our goal extends far beyond the chessboard. We build critical thinking, patience, concentration, and a champion’s mindset in every student—whether you are a beginner or an aspiring competitive player.

Meet Your Coach

Coach Reena Suresh is a passionate chess coach dedicated to helping students unlock their full potential through strategic thinking and disciplined practice.

“Every great chess player starts with a single move. Let’s make yours count.”

A Champion’s Journey

Coach Reena Suresh’s chess journey began at a young age as a District-Level Chess Champion. Her early achievements reflect the dedication, discipline, and passion that inspire her coaching today.

Shaping Tomorrow’s Champions

Coach Reena has voluntarily provided free chess coaching to Government School students, producing Zonal and District-level winners.

Every medal earned is a testament to hard work and excellence.
